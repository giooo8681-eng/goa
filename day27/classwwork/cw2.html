porsche 911

The 2026 Porsche 911 is an iconic rear-engine sports car, available in Carrera, Targa, Turbo, and GT3 variants, starting around \(\$135,000\). It features a 3.0-liter twin-turbo flat-six engine (roughly 388 hp to over 500 hp in higher trims) with a 0-60 mph time as low as 2.9 seconds in GTS models. It is celebrated for combining daily usability with high-performance, precision engineering.
